# FORGE design brief

## Brand thesis

FORGE treats strength training as disciplined work: clear stations, warm concrete, chalk, daylight, and one rust-orange signal replace both luxury wellness softness and black-red gym theater.

## Audience and conversion

Adults in Beirut comparing coached strength and conditioning options. As a fictional Spline concept, the conversion is either completing the safe trial-flow demonstration or contacting Spline about a comparable system.

## Emotional and visual concept

Focused, useful, and unsentimental. The visual metaphor is a working floor at first light: documentary frames, ruled programming tracks, exact zones, and equipment that visibly earns its place.

## Typography, palette, and layout

Condensed heavy display type sits against a neutral workhorse sans. Carbon, chalk, warm concrete, and rust orange provide high contrast. Layouts use long ruled lists, asymmetric media/copy pairs, and a seven-day training surface rather than repeated icon cards.

## Media and material

Generated documentary imagery is explicitly fictional. Desktop hero is wide with left-side copy space; mobile has its own portrait frame. Coach portraits are fictional and carry no credential claims. Concrete, chalk, rubber, and steel are the material vocabulary.

## Motion and interaction

One restrained responsive-media system reacts locally on fine pointers. Schedule, floor-plan, filters, mobile navigation, and pricing states provide functional feedback. Motion never replaces content or runs under reduced motion.

## Mobile transformation

The portrait hero brings action into the lower field, wide schedules become a swipeable day rail, media captions remain readable without hover, and all controls maintain 44 px targets.

## Accessibility and truth

Keyboard navigation, trapped mobile menu focus, focus outlines, semantic tabs, form errors, reduced motion, and safe demo receipts are required. No real facility, person, credential, availability, price, or outcome is implied.

## Anti-pattern blacklist

- Black-and-red fitness cliché
- Bodybuilder close-up or trophy pose
- Luxury wellness card grid
- Fabricated transformations, urgency, credentials, or live capacity
- Hover-only content on touch
- Desktop merely stacked on mobile

## Business-exclusive details

1. The seven-day training rail makes programme rhythm visible.
2. Floor zones connect equipment to purpose and access notes.
3. Membership comparison begins after a trial-flow demonstration.
4. Documentary Beirut light and rust-orange equipment form the visual signature.

## Acceptance criteria

- Hero, schedule, classes, coaches, memberships, facilities, forms, and policies contain no visible placeholders.
- Fictional business and illustrative data are disclosed without dominating the experience.
- Desktop and mobile use separate optimized hero crops.
- Schedule filters, plan periods, floor zones, navigation, forms, WhatsApp, and calendar export work by keyboard and touch.
- Form endpoints return explicit demo receipts and retain no personal data.
- No broken media, dead links, horizontal overflow, or console errors at required viewports.
